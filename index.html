Fix common IT problems yourself, in a few minutes.

Most everyday issues with computers, printers, Wi-Fi and logins can be solved with a few basic steps. Try the guide for your problem first. If it doesn’t work, raise a ticket with the IT team and tell them which steps you already tried.

🧰 Before you call IT

  1. Stay calm and try the simplest fix first: check cables and power.
  2. Restart the app, then the computer.
  3. Note the exact error message or take a screenshot (+Shift+S).
  4. Ask a colleague if they have seen the same issue.
  5. Search this guide for your problem.
  6. Know your limits. If unsure, stop and call IT.

⛔ Never do this yourself

  • Open the computer case or replace internal parts.
  • Install software from unknown websites.
  • Change firewall, antivirus, VPN or network settings.
  • Share your password with anyone, including IT staff.

🎫 When raising a ticket, include

  • Your name, department, floor and asset/PC number.
  • What you were doing when the problem started.
  • The exact error message or a screenshot.
  • Which steps from this guide you already tried.

✅ Daily good habits

  • Restart your computer at least once a week.
  • Save work often and keep office files on the shared drive or OneDrive, not only on the desktop.
  • Install updates when prompted; don’t postpone them for days.
  • Keep laptop vents clear; don’t use it on a bed or cushion.
